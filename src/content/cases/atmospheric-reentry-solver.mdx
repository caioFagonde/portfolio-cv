## Problem

Atmospheric reentry is a coupled systems problem: compressible flow, thermal loading, trajectory design, numerical stiffness, stability, and computational cost all influence each other.

## Direction

This case records the architecture for a high-integrity simulation workflow rather than claiming a production CFD solver. The long-term aim is to express solver assumptions, discretization choices, verification checks, and output artifacts as a reproducible simulation pipeline.

## What matters

- physical assumptions are explicit;
- numerical schemes are documented;
- residuals and convergence are treated as first-class outputs;
- visualizations support engineering interpretation rather than presentation alone.
