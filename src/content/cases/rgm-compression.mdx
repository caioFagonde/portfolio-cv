## Problem

Ordinary visual quality metrics are insufficient for geometry fields. A compression system for depth maps, masks, flow, or scientific rasters must preserve task-relevant structure.

## Direction

The useful question is not only “how small is the file?” It is: did the operation preserve the measurement or decision that matters?

## Bridge

This research line connects naturally to Visual Metrology Studio and Foundry Platform through measurement-preserving processing.
