## Problem

A defect is not just an anomaly. It is a localized violation of expected structure under a set of assumptions.

## Direction

The programme should start with simulation-first benchmarks and evidence-bearing measurements before making claims about repair or certification.

## Near-term bridge

Visual Metrology Studio is the practical on-ramp: measure the defect first, then reason about severity, consistency, growth, and repair.
