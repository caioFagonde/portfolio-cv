## Problem

Professional software systems need more than algorithms. They need artifacts, provenance, metrics, reports, storage contracts, database connectors, workers, permissions, and deployment modes.

## Direction

Foundry Platform is structured as four packages: evidence-core, geo-foundry, vision-foundry, and agent-foundry. The shared rule is simple: every operation emits a typed, auditable artifact.

## Why it matters

This creates a stable backbone for apps that need remote sensing, computer vision, database agents, reports, and model training without becoming a collection of notebooks.
