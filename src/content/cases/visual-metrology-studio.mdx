## Problem

Many image systems produce labels or masks but not defensible measurements. A measurement needs scale, method, uncertainty, evidence overlays, and reproducibility.

## Direction

The first version should measure distances, edge widths, and mask areas on synthetic ground-truth cases, then generate uncertainty-aware HTML and JSON reports.

## Claim discipline

A result is not certified just because it has a number. Certification requires valid input, valid scale, valid method, uncertainty, consistency, and provenance.
