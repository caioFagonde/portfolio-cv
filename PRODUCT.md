# PRODUCT.md — Caio Nahuel Systems Atlas

## Purpose

A living portfolio, CV portal, research log, and technical companion for a systems engineer working across aerospace, numerical simulation, geospatial intelligence, computer vision, software platforms, and agentic systems.

## Audiences

- Technical peers evaluating engineering depth.
- Research collaborators.
- Recruiters and hiring managers.
- Potential clients or partners.
- The author, as a structured memory of ongoing research.

## Site promise

This site shows what was built, what is being researched, what is only conceptual, and how ideas connect.

## Voice

Precise, warm, grounded, uninflated.

## Claims policy

Each case must distinguish:

- concept;
- prototype;
- active research;
- implemented;
- benchmarked;
- production.

Never present speculative research as deployed infrastructure.
